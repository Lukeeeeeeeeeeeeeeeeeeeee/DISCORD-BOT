# Clan Engagement & Operations Guides

## Importance Of Clan To The Member
How do we make the clan important? Well we have to follow a set of steps:
welcoming to the clan (when they join) -> @Helper interacts with them and does things to make them engage with the clan (look at list below) -> helps ask them to come wars

### Engagement:
- Events
- Polls
- General Questions (e.g. "How was your day?", "Best clutch moments?")
- Conversations

### How Do We Connect Them?
Connection is just as important. If a member is friends with the leader, they are far less likely to leave. 
- Build friendships: talk in general, do VCs, DM them to check in.
- Role of @Helper and @Helper+: actively making friends with recruits.

### Why Members Go Inactive
Most members are inactive for the following reasons:
- Mass pings
- No interest in the clan "eg to get better"
- Not knowing of wars (not dmed)
- Not connected to the clan

### Avoiding Inactivity
- Targeted pings to engage (gn everyday, polls every 2 days etc)
- Recruit smart not hard
- DM EVERYONE
- Events as a junior, then we do wars as a member
- Interaction with the clan (gain experience, prioritise the clan, gain activity)

### Enforcing Activity
Without enforcement (expectations or demotions), activity will drop. Promoting inactive people signals that low standards are acceptable.
- Enforced activity leads to harder working staff, greater expectations, and more productivity.
- Demotions after activity checks.
- Slow promotions for staff but lots of hype when they happen.

## Recruitment & Recruiter Coaching

### How to revive inactive recruiters?
1. First, give them a DM. Ask them if they are okay.
2. If they reply, ask them why they haven't been active. If they are struggling, list the "Reasons to Recruit" and give advice.
3. Be friendly, give extra attention, be 1-on-1.
4. Reward them for good work.

### How to welcome a new recruiter?
- Welcome them into the recruiter chat immediately.
- Offer help and response to queries with your own experiences.
- Moderators are expected to teach effective recruiting in steps.

## Inactivity & Revival (General)

### How to revive inactives
- First tag them in discord to see if they respond.
- If no response, DM them personally (e.g., "yo bro where have you been?").
- If they respond: ask to be more active (use friendly terms like "brother" or "legend"), monitor activity, mention events.
- If they don't respond: they are likely a liability; ask leader to kick.

### Making Recruiters Recruit
- Understand blockers: no reward, it's boring.
- Solutions: Gamify (leaderboard), use subroles (Silver Recruiter), tangible rewards.
- Explicit expectations: demotions if they don't meet quotas.

## Loyalty & Morale

### Building Loyalty
Loyalty is built through connection: events, friendships, increased morale, and engagement.

### Morale & Hype
- Morale is driven by language. Avoid "we are losing" or "no one's on". 
- Use "WE ARE WINNING GET ON CENTER" or "WE ARE BACK".
- Hype spreads like a disease. Hype it up in VC too (e.g., "REVOL ON TOP").

## Detailed Guide Pack

### Welcome & Onboarding Guide
Goal: convert a new join into an engaged member within 48–72 hours.
- 0-1h: Auto-welcome (non-ping) + Helper assigned to DM.
- 6h: Friendly DM (verification help, what to do next, invite to next event).
- 0-48h: Verify role, encourage first message, recommend first small action (react to poll).
- 48-72h: Second DM if no activity + flag for review.

### Helper Interaction Playbook
- Tone: Friendly, curious, brief. Use name.
- Avoid "staff speak".
- Targets: DM new joins < 6h, DM unverified < 24h, praise first message.

### Engagement & Events Guide
- micro events (10-30m): Q&A, memewars, 1v1s.
- Repeatable formats: Poll + Conversation.

### Enforcement & Activity Policy
- Enforcement only after attempts to engage.
- Policy flow: Warning 1 (DM) -> Warning 2 (Public Tag) -> Final (Leader review/Kick).
