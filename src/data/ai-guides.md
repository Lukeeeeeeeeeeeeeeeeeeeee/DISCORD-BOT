# Clan Engagement & Operations Guides

## Importance Of Clan To The Member
How do we make the clan important? Follow a set of steps:
- welcoming to the clan (when they join)
- @Helper interacts with them and does things to make them engage
- helps ask them to come wars

### Engagement
- Events
- Polls
- General Questions
- Conversations

### Connection
Connection is as important as engagement. If members are friends with leaders or staff, loyalty increases. Build friendships by talking in general, doing VCs, DMs, and checking in.

### Why Members Go Inactive
Common reasons:
- Mass pings
- No interest in the clan / no goal
- Not knowing about wars (not DMed)
- Not connected to the clan

### Avoiding Inactivity
- Targeted pings to engage (e.g. GN daily, polls every 2 days)
- Recruit smart, not hard
- DM everyone
- Events as a junior, then wars as a member
- Interaction with the clan

### Why Enforce Activity
Without enforcement, expectations fall. Promoting inactive people signals low standards. Enforcement leads to higher productivity and more clan activity.

### Enforcing Activity
- Demotions after activity checks
- Slow promotions for staff but lots of hype with them when they happen

## Recruitment & Recruiter Coaching

### How To Revive Inactive Recruiters
1) DM them and ask if they're okay.
2) If they reply, ask why they’ve been inactive and help them with recruiting tips.
3) Be friendly, give attention, 1-on-1 coaching.
4) Reward good work when they get recruits.

### How To Welcome New Recruiters
- Welcome in recruiter chat (short friendly welcome)
- Offer help and point to resources
- Mentorship increases activity and recruiting results

### How To Revive Inactives (General)
1) Tag them publicly to see if they respond.
2) DM if no response.
3) If no response after follow-ups: escalate to leader for review.

### Making Recruiters Recruit
Main blockers:
- No reward
- It’s boring

Solutions:
- Gamify recruiting (leaderboards, subroles)
- Provide tangible rewards
- Use reasons-to-recruit
- Set clear expectations and demotion after attempts to re-engage

## Loyalty & Morale

### Building Loyalty
- Events
- Building friendships
- Increase morale and clan pride
- Engagement

- Use positive language ("We’re winning — get on center!")

## Guide Pack — Clan Engagement & Operations

### Welcome & Onboarding Guide
Goal: convert a new join into an engaged member within 48–72 hours.

**Immediate actions (0–1 hour)**
- Auto-welcome message (non-pinging): short + link to verify
- Assign a helper: DM within first hour

**First contact (within 6 hours)**
- Friendly DM + verification help + invite to next event
- Post a welcome in recruiter/recruiter-log (helper assigned)

**0–48 hour checklist**
- Verify role added
- Encourage first message
- Recommend a small action (poll reaction / 15-min VC)

**48–72 hour follow-up**
- If unverified/no activity: second DM + event link
- If still no contact: flag as cold-join

### Helper Interaction Playbook
- Friendly, curious, brief tone
- Use their name
- Use personal touches
- Avoid stiff staff speech

**Flow**
1) First DM: welcome + verification help + 1 quick ask
2) Second DM after 24h if no reply
3) Public nudge only if member consents

**Targets**
- DM new joins within 6 hours
- DM unverified after 24h
- Public praise for first message

### Engagement & Events Guide
Low-friction events:
- Q&A, memewars, quick 1v1s
- Weekly recurring: hype war, newbie night
- Monthly larger events

**Timing**
- Run during peak times
- Target invites to specific cohorts

**Repeatable formats**
- Poll + 1-question follow-up
- 20-min VC hang with a prompt
- Bring-a-friend nights

**Measurement**
- RSVPs, attendance, messages during event, retention of attendees

### Recruitment & Recruiter Incentives
- Leaderboards by invites-used, verified recruits, quality recruits
- Subroles: Bronze / Silver / Gold
- Rewards: roles, shoutouts, small prizes
- Monthly top recruiter recognition

### Inactivity & Revival Guide
Flags: inactive_14d (not absent), unverified_48h, recruiter_inactive_7d

**Revival flow**
1) Public nudge (positive)
2) Personal DM
3) Invite to small event
4) After 7–10 days: leader review

### Morale & Hype Guide
- Use action-oriented, positive language
- Use "we" language
- Avoid blame culture in public chats
- Avoid mass pings

### Enforcement & Activity Policy
- Enforcement only after attempts to engage
- Demotions tied to published metrics

**Policy flow**
- Warning 1: DM + 7-day monitoring
- Warning 2: public/tag + offer of help
- Final: leader review → demotion/kick

### Staff Training & Mentoring Guide
- Short workshop on DM/event/welcome
- Buddy system for new staff
- Weekly 15-min check-ins
- Positive-first feedback

### DM Templates
**Casual welcome (first hour)**
Hey [name]! Welcome to [Clan]. I’m [HelperName] — if you need help verifying or want to jump into a quick voice hang, ping me. What games do you play?

**Verification nudge (24–48h)**
Hey [name], quick reminder — if you haven’t verified yet, here’s the link: [link]. If you need help I can walk you through it now.

**Revival DM (inactive)**
Yo [name], we noticed you’ve been quiet — everything okay? We’ve got a chill hang tonight at [time], would be great to see you.

**Recruiter coaching (private)**
Hey [recruiter], noticed things have been slow — want a 10-min tip session? A few tweaks in your opener could boost replies.

**Demotion notice (kind, factual)**
Hi [name], we reviewed activity and you’ve been inactive for X days despite messages. Per policy, we’re pausing recruiter duties. If you want to be reactivated, reply here and we’ll help.

### Event & Poll Templates
**Poll idea (every 2 days)**
Poll: Which topic should we cover this week?
• A: War tips
• B: New recruit Q&A
• C: Gameplay clips

**Quick event announcement**
20-min VC hang at [time] — topic: “best clutch moments.” Drop a ✅ to join. Helpers will hop in to meet new faces.

**Recruitment challenge**
48-hour leaderboard: top 3 recruiters get Silver Recruiter role and shoutout.
